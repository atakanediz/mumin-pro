Mümin Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

In short: No account needed. No ads. We do not track you and we do not sell your data. What you read, bookmark and plan stays on your phone — we run no user server.

1. No account

Using Mümin Pro requires no sign-up, e-mail address or phone number.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your prayer settings, selected and saved cities, bookmarks, last read position, reading log and streak, reading plans, tasbih and dhikr records, and any memorisation recordings you make with the microphone are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it; we keep no copy, because it never reached us.

3. Location

If you grant permission, your location is used to calculate prayer times, find the qibla and — when you ask for it — search for nearby mosques. Your location is never sent to a server of ours. It does reach Apple in two cases: resolving your city name (Apple's geocoding service) and searching for nearby mosques (Apple Maps). Apple's privacy policy applies to those requests. You can also pick a city manually and grant no location access at all.

4. Anonymous usage measurement

We use TelemetryDeck to understand which parts of the app are used. Signals say which screen was opened, not who you are. No advertising identifier is used, and because the app does not track you across apps or websites, no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

5. Bug reports

Only if you submit the in-app "Report a problem" form do we receive: the text you wrote, a screenshot if you attach one, your e-mail address if you choose to give one, and the app version, iOS version, device model, language and the screen you opened the form from. Your location, contacts and device identifier are not sent. The report travels through a Cloudflare Workers endpoint and reaches the developer over Telegram; it is used only to fix the problem you reported.

6. Live broadcast

If you open the live broadcast section, video is played through youtube-nocookie.com and your IP address reaches Google, under Google's privacy policy. Not opening that section creates no such connection.

7. Notifications

Prayer and holy-day notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved, so we hold no push token for you.

8. Selling and sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising. Apart from the services named above, we transfer data to no third party.

9. Children

The app is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can access, correct and delete it directly in the app. To request deletion of a record sent through the feedback form, write to the address below.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and significant changes are announced in the app.

12. Contact

Contact: muminpro.help@gmail.com
You can also write from More › Report a problem in the app.
