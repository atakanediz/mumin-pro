Support

Fastest route: from inside the app

Use More › Report a problem. The form attaches the technical details needed to understand the issue (app and iOS version, device model, the screen you were on), so no back-and-forth is needed. You can attach a screenshot too.

Common questions

Prayer times differ from my local authority. Times depend on your location and the calculation method. Check method and madhab under Prayer › Settings; you can also set a per-prayer minute adjustment. If the city is wrong, tap the city chip and pick it manually.

Notifications don't arrive. Check iOS Settings › Notifications for Mümin Pro, then make sure the bell for that prayer is on under Prayer › Settings › Notifications. Focus modes can delay delivery.

The qibla seems off. The compass uses the phone's magnetometer. Calibrate by moving the phone in a figure of eight, away from metal surfaces and magnetic cases.

How do I delete my data? Everything stays on your phone; removing the app deletes all of it. See the privacy policy.
